Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This page describes what the current Hydration Buddy app does with information. The app has no sign-in, user account, analytics, advertising, or cloud sync.

Information saved on your device

Your optional name, drink entries, daily goal, and reminder preferences are saved in this browser on this device. The app author does not receive these entries. They do not automatically move to another device. You can remove individual drink entries in the app or clear this site's stored data in your browser settings.

When information leaves this device

  • Weather: If you request a city lookup, that city is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding and weather services so the app can show local conditions.
  • Voice: Voice input is optional. If you choose the microphone button, your browser's speech recognition service may process the audio. Availability and processing vary by browser and device.
  • Notifications: Browser notifications are optional and requested only when you choose Enable notifications. Browser and operating-system settings control permission.
  • Hosting: The website is hosted by Netlify. Like other website hosts, it may process technical connection information to deliver the site under its own policies.

Offline storage

After the first successful visit, the app may cache its static files on this device so the interface can open offline. Weather lookups need an internet connection. Clearing the site's data in browser settings removes the cached files and locally saved app information.

Support

For a question or to report a problem, use Hydration Buddy support on GitHub. GitHub issues may be public. Please do not post health details or other private information there.

Health reminder: Drink logs can reveal personal health habits. This app keeps them in your browser storage, which may be accessible to other people who use your device or browser profile.
