General guide: 1.6 L (8 × 200 ml drinks); on very hot days, this app uses 2 L as a higher planning guide. NHS advice says needs vary and people may need more in heat, but it does not set a fixed heat increase. If your diabetes team has set a target or you have a fluid limit, enter that target instead. Check with your care team if you have kidney or heart problems. This tracker is not medical advice.

Privacy: your drink log and goal stay in this browser on this device. Weather searches send the city you enter to Open-Meteo. If you use the microphone, your browser's speech recognition service may process audio; voice is optional and behavior varies by browser.

Reminders and alarms work while this page remains open.
