About Hydration Buddy

A friendly, flexible tool for logging drinks, checking local weather, and building a hydration routine. Choose a reminder interval and active hours that suit your day.

How it works

  • Your drink log and goal stay in this browser on this device.
  • You can edit or remove drink entries and set a goal that reflects your care team's advice.
  • Weather can inform the general planning guide, but the app uses a simple guide and cannot assess your individual needs.
  • Reminders run only while the page remains open. Browser notifications are optional.

Health guidance

The general guide is a starting point, not a prescription. Hydration needs vary with activity, weather, health, and medicines. The app's hot-weather adjustment is a planning prompt, not a medical recommendation. It does not lower the guide in cold weather.

Follow your own care plan. If your care team has set a fluid target or limit, use that. Check with them before changing fluid intake if you have diabetes, kidney or heart problems, or take medicines that affect fluid balance.

This app does not diagnose, treat, or monitor a medical condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you feel seriously unwell, seek medical help.

Support

Report a bug or request an improvement through Hydration Buddy support on GitHub. Please do not include private health information in public issues.
